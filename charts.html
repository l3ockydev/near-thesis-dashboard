An investment thesis is only as good as its evidence. This note tests one claim about NEAR — that growth in real onchain capital tells a story the token price alone does not — using public data only, in a pipeline that rebuilds every figure below on demand. The claim, the counter-evidence, and what would falsify it are stated plainly in THESIS.md.

Figure 1 · Chain TVL, 2021–present

Momentum is back. Four years of deficit is still the burden of proof.

Total value locked — capital actively committed to NEAR applications — reads $169.7M: up +83.7% in 90 days, and still 64.8% below the peak of May 2022. A thesis that quotes only one of the two numbers is selling something other than an analysis.

Source: DefiLlama chain TVL. Computed at build from data/chain_tvl.json — snapshot included in the repo.

Figure 2 · Stablecoins on NEAR, 2022–present

Recorded dollars barely move. This is the weakest leg of the long case.

Stablecoin balances are demand that stays after the trading stops. The series reads $130.5M — changed just +1.9% in 90 days against a longer, flatter history. It stands unexplained here on purpose; a claim cannot be bullish on autopilot.

Source: DefiLlama stablecoins per chain (stablecoincharts/NEAR). Note the scale: this chart is smaller than a single notch of Figure 1.

Figure 3 · NEAR token price, daily closes

Price is a verdict, not evidence.

The token's daily close ($3.64 at this build) is kept in view throughout for comparison. It is treated as an outcome to be explained — never as input of proof. Free CoinGecko data covers 365 days; that is why the paired figures below are window views, not full history.

Source: CoinGecko free public API. Rate-limited; when it fails, the build degrades honestly to TVL and stablecoins only.

Figure 4 · Market cap and chain TVL, paired

The long case collapses into one multiple.

Market cap is what speculation believes; TVL is what capital does. Over the 365-day overlap the market pays about $28 of market cap per $1 of working capital (TVL/MC 0.036 (3.6%), against a window peak of 0.065 (6.5%)). Whether that multiple compresses or expands is the investment thesis, stated in one number.

Method. One command — node rebuild.mjs — refetches every series and re-renders all figures above: DefiLlama for chain TVL and NEAR stablecoins, CoinGecko free tier for price and market cap (365-day window), NEAR public mainnet RPC for the liveness check. Metrics that genuinely cannot be computed for zero cost — daily active accounts, per-contract usage, Intents swap volume — are not substituted with estimates; the corresponding BigQuery SQL ships in sql/ so anyone can produce them. Caveats. Correlation is not causation; TVL methodology is DefiLlama's, not this note's; CoinGecko's free window is 365 days. See README "Methodology notes" for the data-quality bug found and documented mid-build.
